What StoryCV does

It does what a good resume writer does first. It asks.

The interview

One role at a time, StoryCV asks what you owned, what you changed, and what happened next. When an answer comes back vague, it asks again. (Politely. Every time.)

The bullet points

Your answers become bullet points you can defend. Every number in them came from you, so when an interviewer says "tell me more about that," you've already told it once.

The Career Story

Everything you say gets saved. Next application, you start from what you've already dug up, instead of a blinking cursor and a hazy memory of 2021.

How StoryCV works

Six steps. The longest one is finding your old resume.

  1. Sign up free

    Head to write.story.cv. No card needed.

  2. Upload your resume

    StoryCV reads it and only asks about what's missing. (No resume? Start from scratch. You'll just answer a few more questions.)

  3. Name the role you want

    So the interview knows where to dig.

  4. Answer one role at a time

    Typing or talking. About 10 to 15 minutes each.

  5. Review your bullets

    Fix anything that doesn't sound like you, then copy them into your resume template. (Full formatted export is on the way.)

  6. Come back anytime

    Your Career Story is saved, so the next application starts where this one left off.

Frequently asked questions

The things people ask before their first interview.

Is StoryCV an automatic CV writer?

Partly. The writing is automatic, but StoryCV won't write until it's interviewed you, because a CV built on vague input comes out vague. You bring the stories, it does the wording.

Can I use ChatGPT for resume writing instead?

You can, but ChatGPT rewrites whatever you give it, so thin input comes back as polished thin output. StoryCV interviews you first and won't write a bullet until it has the problem, the action and the result. It also remembers your answers for the next application.

How is StoryCV different from resume writing services online?

Most resume writing services online sell you a human writer's time, usually US$350 to $700 and several days of waiting. StoryCV runs the same kind of interview for a fraction of the price, and you walk away with bullets the same day. Your answers stay with you, so you're not paying again every time you apply.

What should I look for in the best CV writing websites?

Look for one that asks about your work instead of handing you a template to fill. The best AI resume writer is the one that drags specifics out of you (numbers, scope, results), because that's what a hiring manager reads for.

Do I need an existing resume to start?

It helps. Upload your current resume and StoryCV only asks about what's missing. You can start from scratch, but you'll answer more questions along the way.

How long does it take?

About 10 to 15 minutes per role, and faster if you speak your answers instead of typing. Most people finish a full resume in 1 to 1.5 hours in one sitting.

Do I get a formatted resume at the end?

For now, you copy your bullet points into your own resume template. Full formatted resume export is on the way.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your answers and your Career Story are private to you. Read the full privacy policy.

Will my resume get past ATS?

StoryCV writes clear, plain-text bullet points that applicant tracking systems read without trouble. We'd still rather you write for the person reading it after the ATS, since they're the one who calls you back.
