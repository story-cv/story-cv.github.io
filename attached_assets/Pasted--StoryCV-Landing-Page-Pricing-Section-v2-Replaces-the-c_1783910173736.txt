- Two lines, centered, breaking after "week,"

## Layout

Two cards, side by side, equal width, centered. **Both cards have the same neutral hairline border** — no accent border, no badge, no "most popular" treatment on either card. The only visual emphasis on the paid card is its filled (brand orange) CTA button; the free card's CTA is an outline/ghost button.

On mobile, cards stack vertically: The First Chapter first, The Full Story second.

Below the cards: a short prose note (see "Philosophy note"), left-aligned, max-width ~480px, centered as a block.

## Philosophy note (below the cards)

**Heading (small, bold):** Why a pass and not a subscription?

**Body (two paragraphs, left-aligned):**

> Rewriting your resume is a project. It has an end. Subscriptions make money by assuming you'll forget to cancel (we'd rather not build a business on that).
>
> So you pay for the week you're actually working. When it ends, your bullets are yours forever. Need it again next year? Come back. Same price.

## Design notes

- Keep the existing StoryCV palette: cream section background, dark ink text, brand orange accent, white cards with rounded corners (~12px).
- Keep existing StoryCV fonts exactly as they are on the current site.
- Remove from the current design: the strikethrough $59, the "Founding User Price" badge, the italic taglines under each card, and the subheading "Your career has a story. Tell it well."
- Checklist icons: simple check marks in brand orange, not filled circles.
- No feature-comparison table, no toggle, no third tier.

## Copy rules (do not change these in implementation)

- CTA wording is exact: "Start free" and "Get my pass" — do not substitute "Sign up", "Upgrade", "Unlock", or similar.
- "one-week pass" is always lowercase, always hyphenated.
- Do not add exclamation marks anywhere in this section.
- Do not add a money-back guarantee line to the cards (it lives in the FAQ only).
