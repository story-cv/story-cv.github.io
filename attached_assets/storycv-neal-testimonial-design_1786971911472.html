The decisions

  • Square photo, not a circle. Circles are avatar language and shrink a good portrait into a comment-thread element.
  • Photo sits flush to the card edges with zero padding. The bleed is what makes it editorial.
  • Warm sand panel behind the image with multiply blending, so his cool grey studio background dissolves instead of clashing with your cream.
  • Crop positioned at 22% from the top so his head sits in the upper third. Dead-centre framing looks like an ID card.
  • Quote at weight 500, not 800. Heavy type at 26px shouts. Lighter weight at large size reads confident.
  • Card radius 4px, not 14px. Small radii read as print, large radii read as app UI.
  • Institution in 11px uppercase with wide tracking. The jump from 26px to 11px is the move.
  • No shadow, no border. White on cream separates on its own.
