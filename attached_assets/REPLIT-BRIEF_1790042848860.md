# New landing page: `/guided-interview-resume-tool`

## What I want

A landing page for people searching "guided interview resume tool," "conversational resume writer" and "digital resume writer." All three mean the same thing: a tool that interviews you and writes your bullets. One page makes that case, instead of three posts competing with each other.

These visitors already know the kind of tool they want. They're comparing options. So the page leads with a demo, not a problem. It shows an interview happening, shows how one flat answer turns into a strong bullet, and makes the method visible: StoryCV follows a human resume writer's workflow in digital form. It starts from the target role, compares it with what it already knows about the person, asks about the gaps, and checks every answer for seven things. Then it compares StoryCV to the alternatives.

Order: hero with a live interview → stakes (most "guided" tools are forms) → one answer dug down three levels → founder video → the resume writer's workflow (gap-analysis card, then the seven signals) → testimonial → three-step plan → comparison table → objections → pricing → CTA → FAQ → related reading.

Motion is subtle and in service of the story. Same design language as `/chatgpt-for-resumes`.

## The four things that must survive

However you restructure the CSS, check these:

1. **The hero chat card never jumps in height.** The typing dots and the follow-up question ("What made it hard?") share one slot and crossfade. If they're built as two stacked elements, the card grows when the question appears.

2. **"Record and transcribe" / "Listening…" stay centred under the mic.** Don't centre them with `translateX(-50%)` on the same element the crossfade animates with `transform: scale()`. The animation overwrites the translate and the label drifts right. (Same fix as the ChatGPT page.)

3. **Nothing can get stuck invisible.** The stakes headline's word-by-word reveal and the gap-analysis rows both trigger off the `.visible` class that `scroll-animation.js` adds, never `animation-timeline: view()`. (Same fix as the ChatGPT page.)

4. **The comparison table scrolls sideways inside its own box on phones.** The page itself must never scroll horizontally at 390px.
